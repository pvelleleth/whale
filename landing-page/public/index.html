FAQs

Common questions.

Is Whale really free?+

Whale is free to download and use, and always will be. AI processing requires your own OpenAI API key, and OpenAI charges your account for that usage. Recording itself doesn't require a key.

Which Macs does it work on?+

This download supports Apple Silicon Macs (M-series chips) running macOS 15 or later. It doesn't include an Intel build. Check your Mac under Apple menu → About This Mac.

What does “invisible” mean?+

Whale doesn't add a meeting bot or floating recording pill. Recording continues with its window closed. macOS recording indicators remain visible, and an open Whale window can appear when sharing your screen.

Does a bot join my meeting?+

No. Whale records your microphone and your Mac's audio locally. It doesn't join the call as another participant. macOS recording indicators remain visible. Let other participants know you're recording.

Is everything processed on my Mac?+

Recording, storage, and echo reduction happen on your Mac. Transcription uploads audio to OpenAI, and summaries send transcript content to OpenAI using your API key. The app doesn't offer a fully offline transcription mode.

Can I use it with my usual meeting app?+

Whale captures system audio rather than relying on a meeting-app integration. It records all Mac audio except its own output, so pause unrelated audio before recording. Try a short test call with your setup first.

Can I take my notes with me?+

Yes. Export your notes as Markdown, search your meeting library, and open the saved audio in Finder. Original recordings are kept alongside derived audio until you delete the meeting.
